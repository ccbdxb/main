The Story

Mazement — Where mazes meet meaning

Where digital mazes meet physical art — a phygital journey through creativity, logic, and chance.

A Byrne family collaboration between artist & printmaker Ricky Byrne and programmer Clinton Byrne.

Mazement bridges the gap between physical and digital art — where each maze represents the human pursuit of goals: love, safety, meaning, adventure.

The mazes serve as a metaphor for our very human struggle to find that 'thing' we're working towards. Our need for goals is universal, our desire to reach them fundamentally human. It's no surprise the labyrinth — and later the maze — is a persistent feature of human culture and history.

Digital precision meets human imperfection.

🎨 Minting Experience — Phygital Art

Every Mazement NFT minted comes with the opportunity to redeem a hand-crafted physical maze artwork, created by rbyrneart.com.

Each design is produced in limited-edition batches of 10. Once a design's run is complete, a new maze is created for the next batch — ensuring continuous artistic evolution.

Once the mint window closes, the physical claim can no longer be made by secondary buyers — making this reward exclusive to original minters.
