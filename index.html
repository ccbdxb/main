The Story

Mazement bridges the gap between physical and digital art. Each maze represents the human pursuit of goals — love, safety, meaning, adventure.

The mazes serve as a metaphor for our very human struggle to find that 'thing' we are working towards. Our need for goals is universal, our desire to reach them fundamentally human.

It's no surprise the labyrinth and later the maze are persistent features of human culture, regardless of where and when we are from. Digital precision meets human imperfection.

How It Works

Every Mazement NFT comes with one-time physical redemption. Artist Ricky Byrne creates handcrafted maze artworks in limited batches of 10 pieces per design.

Once a design's run is complete, a new maze is created for the next batch — ensuring continuous artistic evolution. The physical artwork is not a replica of your NFT, but a unique piece within a small-edition series.

Physical redemption is exclusive to original minters. Once the mint window closes, secondary buyers cannot claim the physical artwork.
